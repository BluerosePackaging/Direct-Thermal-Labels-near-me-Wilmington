## Print Labels Without Ink or Toner

One of the main advantages of direct thermal printing is its straightforward printing process. Heat from the printer creates the required image directly on the label material, eliminating the need for ink cartridges or ribbons. This can simplify routine labeling operations and reduce the number of consumable components required for printing.

This makes direct thermal labels especially useful where shipping labels, barcodes, dates, tracking numbers, and other variable information need to be produced quickly and consistently.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are direct thermal labels?

Direct thermal labels use heat-sensitive material that reacts to a thermal printer's print head to create text, barcodes, and other information. They do not require conventional ink or toner.

### Are direct thermal labels suitable for shipping?

Yes. Direct thermal labels are widely used for shipping information, tracking numbers, addresses, and barcodes. BlueRose specifically lists shipping and logistics among their applications.

### Can I get custom-sized direct thermal labels?

### Are direct thermal labels available for cold environments?

Yes. BlueRose offers freezer-grade direct thermal labels designed with adhesive performance for demanding temperature conditions.

### Do direct thermal labels require a ribbon?

No. Direct thermal printing creates the image directly on heat-sensitive label material, so a conventional thermal transfer ribbon is not required.
